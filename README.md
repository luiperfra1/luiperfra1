## About me

I'm an **AI Researcher and Software Engineer** based in Seville, Spain, currently working at the **University of Seville**.

My work sits at the intersection of **artificial intelligence research and production-oriented software engineering**. I am particularly interested in building, evaluating and understanding reliable AI systems based on language models, structured knowledge and modern NLP.

- Researching **LLMs, hallucination detection, model evaluation and reliable AI**
- Working with **NLP, RAG and Knowledge Graphs**
- Building experimental pipelines and AI-backed applications from prototype to usable software
- Interested in rigorous evaluation, reproducible experimentation and applied research
